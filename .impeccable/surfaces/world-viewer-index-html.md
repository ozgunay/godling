# Viewer screen (the HUD over the live island)

Scope: the screen people see when they watch Godling, on the website (phones and desktops) and in the 16:9 restream. Visitor mode: Experience (they watch a living work and reach into it). Prototype first: screenshots over real captures, then the live build inside the renderer page.

Audience and job: drop-ins understand in seconds who Godling is, what it is doing and that they can change its world; regulars read its needs, history and feats. Constraints: the 3D view leads; phone-light; prices shown in credits and euros; the agent never sees who paid; keep the free camera, with WASD flying it in free mode (user, 2026-11-02); the style is Godling's own, never a copy of a famous block game (user, 2026-11-02).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Speak the block-game HUD every young viewer already reads, built from Godling's own island: powers are a hotbar of island blocks, its body is hearts and coconuts, its mind is the feed, its feats are toasts. Refuses the stream sidebar of glass cards.
OWN-WORLD: Slots are slate island stone with a moss lip and a hard bevel; halo gold marks only what a viewer can press and what the sky did; lagoon teal marks what Godling noticed; white pixel type with a one-pixel shadow; authored 16-pixel icons of the island's own things.
STORY: The visitor sees Godling working, reads its latest line, sees what it needs, and drops a log from the hotbar; the feed and a toast show Godling noticing it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Live island full-bleed. Top-centre LIVE · N watching, the loudest thing at the top (user, 2026-11-02). Top-left Godling's mind, folded like a game's quest tracker (user, 2026-11-03): the newest thought in two lines, what it is doing now (or that it stands thinking) and what it needs, with each new line shown a while and fading; it unfolds to the feed's five lines, and any cut line opens in full. Top-right day, hour, weather and the camera switch (Follow Godling / Free camera, WASD). Right, under them, pop-ups of what viewers did (user, 2026-11-02). Bottom-centre hearts and coconuts above a seven-slot hotbar of the world's powers and the note, prices in credits under each, cooldown sweeps: the primary action; picking a power aims it on the island (user, 2026-11-02). The purse beside the hotbar.
FORM: Block HUD, first of my seven grounded candidates (my pick, chosen by the user); seed key 61beadb2.
