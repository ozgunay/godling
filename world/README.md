# Godling's Mesa Island

A copy of Clawblox's `worlds/mesa-island` (MIT, github.com/nacloos/clawblox at `8be90a0`), changed for Godling. Runs copy this folder into their run directory (`run_godling.py`, `prepare_world`). The only per-run change is the walk speed.

## Changes from upstream
- **The castaway island (your wish, after Cast Away; ADR-005).** The upstream island and its mesas are replaced. (The island as it is now is generated by `tools/make_island.py`: ADR-007, enlarged with meadows in ADR-009. The lines below describe the first version.)
  - **Ground.** A sand beach all round, widest in the south, lies 0.1 below the grass inland (`Surface` "Sand" or "Grass" on `Ground` parts). There is a palm islet across a strait in the south-east.
  - **The peak.** A rocky peak in the north-west: `PeakBase` 5, `PeakShoulder` 10, `PeakSummit` 15 and `PeakCrag` 20, each one ladder-climb apart. Low rocks are `WestSpur`, `FlatRock` and `Ridge`, all `Mesa` with `Surface` "Rock".
  - **Quarry and trees.** The quarry is cut into the peak's south foot. Eight trees form a forest in the east. Ten coconut palms (`Palm_1`…`Palm_10`, `palm.glb`, quarter-turned to lean seaward) line the beaches and the islet; felled, they give logs.
  - **Spawn.** Godling washes up on the west beach beside `Raft`, a busted yellow life raft (`RenderRole` "Wreck"; the body routes round it).
- **Minecraft-scale blocks on a grid (ADR-005).**
  - `BLOCK_SIZE` is 2.5 (upstream 0.75): half the 5-tall body.
  - Placed blocks and logs snap to a grid of 2.5 cells (x and z).
  - The 12 starting blocks lie flat on the grid and are stone-heavy.
  - The rock's impact blocks are off.
  - Logs are three-block beams, and the ladder is three blocks tall.
  - The sizes below that predate ADR-005 have been rescaled in `main.lua`.
- **Human-paced body (ADR-003 O2).**
  - A picked-up block rises into the hands over `LIFT_SECONDS` (1.0 s). Upstream it snaps there.
  - A placed block is lowered into place over `PLACE_SECONDS` (1.2 s). Upstream it takes 0.3 s.
  - Walking pace is set per run (`--walk-speed`, default 5).
  - The jump is a person's hop: `JUMP_POWER` 15 lifts the body about 0.6. Upstream's jump is about 6.6.
  - The mind's `godling/world.py` mirrors these constants.
- **Building (ADR-004).** The ways up are ladders and building, not jumping. Since ADR-010 the body also steps up onto, and down off, anything one block high (`StepUp`/`StepDown {position}`), when it fits there.
  - **Quarry.** An outcrop (`Quarry`, 6 x 3.5 x 5 at (28, -40)), with `Hammer_1` lying next to it.
    - Each `Strike` with the hammer in hand counts toward the next stone. Every `STRIKES_PER_STONE` (3) strikes, a stone breaks off: a `Stone_n` cube (0.75) and a `Brick_n` (1.5 x 0.75 x 0.75) by turns.
    - Stones are heavy and grippy (density 2.6, friction 1.0). The quarry never runs out.
  - **Trees.** `Axe_1` lies by the grove.
    - `CHOPS_PER_TREE` (4) strikes with the axe fell a tree into two `Log_n` beams (light wood), lying along the fall. A stump is left.
    - Logs are 7.5 x 2.5 x 2.5: square beams three blocks long and one thick, so they fit the cube grid. Round ones rolled off what they were set on.
  - **Settling (ADR-005).** A block, stone or log that has come to rest lying on a face is squared into its grid cell and held there (anchored, attribute `Settled`), so the body cannot shove it. Lifting a piece wakes what rested on it; that falls until it settles again.
  - **Touching is not overlap.** The placement probe is 0.1 smaller than the piece, because grid neighbours always touch.
    - The tree grows back after `TREE_REGROW_SECONDS` (600).
  - **Placing.** `Place` takes a rotation, as upstream. The body uses it to turn pieces a quarter round and to stand long pieces on end.
  - **Fastening.** `Fasten {name}` fixes a set-down piece that touches something where it is (`Anchored`, `Fastened`), like mortar, after a `FASTEN_COOLDOWN` (2.5 s) between fastenings.
    - Unlike a settled piece, a fastened one stays even when what it rests on is taken away.
    - Nothing is welded. In the first night run, blocks welded to `GroundCenter` made the engine move the ground: the island sank 5 units and Godling fell into the sea.
    - A fastened piece cannot be picked up. A hammer strike knocks it loose; it then settles, or falls if nothing holds it up.
  - **Ladder.** `Ladder_1` (1.0 x 4.5 x 0.3) is carried lying flat.
    - Set down, it always stands upright (only its facing turns) and is anchored.
    - `Climb {name}` from its foot moves the body up and steps it off onto the highest surface the ladder reaches, on either side. From up on something along its height, `Climb` moves the body back down.
    - The engine has no climbing, so the world moves the anchored root part, and the player attribute `Climbing` is true meanwhile.
  - **Boulders.** `Boulder_1` and `Boulder_2`: balls 1.6 across, stone-heavy. Carrying one halves the walking pace (`BaseWalkSpeed` / 2).
  - **Keepsakes.** Tools, the ladder and the boulders turn up again at home if lost in the sea.
  - **Stone yard (ADR-005).** A stone breaks off onto the free ground cell nearest the struck face, in the ring of cells around the quarry's foot (24 cells), never onto the body.
    - When every yard cell holds a stone, `Strike` answers `StrikeYardFull` and the blow is not counted. Strewn stones once boxed Godling in (night run n2).
    - `Hammer_1` lies just outside the ring, at (16, -34).
  - **Spawning.** New logs appear at a free spot (`freeSpawn`), along the fall or higher up. They never appear inside another piece.
  - **Sky supply.** The cube supply from the sky (ADR-003 O7) is off (`SUPPLY_FROM_SKY = false`). The quarry replaces it; the code stays for viewer drops.
- **Godling's look (art session; build scripts and previews in `art/`, details in `art/README.md`).**
  - The character uses `assets/godling.glb`, set as `ModelUrl` on the root part. It has Idle and Walk, a gesture clip for each action the world names (PickUp, PutDown, Throw, Chop, Mine, Carve, Spear, Shake, Kindle, Cook, Eat, Fasten, Draw, Talk, WakeUp), the Jump, Fall and Climb loops, and Strike (see `art/README.md`).
  - Eight trees, `Tree_1`…`Tree_8`: anchored trunk colliders (2.5 x 20 x 2.5, on grid cells about 10 apart) with `RenderRole` "Tree", drawn with `assets/tree.glb`.
    - A felled tree falls along the grid into two logs side by side, in the cells next to its stump (2.5 x 1.25 x 2.5, `RenderRole` "Stump"). The body walks round stumps as terrain.
  - **Terrain on the block grid (ADR-005).** Mesa and quarry heights are whole blocks (2.5, 5, 7.5, 10, 20), and their faces lie on cell boundaries, so blocks set against them sit flush.
    - The mind treats trees and the quarry as terrain too high to climb (`godling/world.py`).
  - The `ART` table in `main.lua` maps objects to `assets/*.glb`: hammer, quarry, stone, brick, axe, log and ladder. Boulders have no model yet.
  - Player attributes for the renderer, with `LastActionResult` unchanged:
    - `Reaches` (pick-ups, placements and fastenings) plays PickUp;
    - `Strikes` plays Strike;
    - `Climbing` loops Climb.
- **Face.** `Face {position}` turns the body on the spot towards a point (`TURN_RATE` 6 rad/s), without stepping anywhere. The mind's glance reflex uses it. A `MoveTo` cancels a turn.
- **Sky events and the guest panel** (research report "God games lessons for Godling", batch 1).
  - Five powers, `SKY_POWERS`:
    - `Stone` and `Log` drop a piece from 12 up.
    - `Regrow` grows the stump nearest Godling back at once.
    - `Lightning` fells the tree nearest Godling into two logs, through the axe's `fellTree`.
    - `Meteor` lays a `SkyShadow_n` on the ground; `SKY_WARN_SECONDS` (40) later a dark `Meteor_n` stone falls into it. It waits while anyone stands within 3 of the shadow's centre.
  - Viewers choose what; the world chooses where. Drops land 8–16 from Godling, on open ground, at least 4 from any building piece.
  - Every piece a power leaves carries `SkyEvent` (its kind) and `SkyId`. The world log prints a `SKY <kind> #<id>` line for each.
  - Powers come only from the bodiless player `Sky` (`SKY_PLAYER`), which `tools/guest_panel.py` joins as. It is parked under the island, and the mind leaves it out of what it sees. `max_players` is 2 for it.
  - `SKY_TEST_EVERY` (seconds, 0 = off) drops a meteor near Godling on a timer, to measure reactions without the panel. `run_godling.py --sky-test-minutes` sets it.
- **Earthquake** (`sky.quake`; plan: `research/god-powers-and-disasters-plan.md` §5). A guest-panel power, `Earthquake`.
  - **Phases:** an 8 s rumble, 20 s of shaking, a 5 s fade. The Clock carries `Quake` (0–1), `QuakePhase` ("rumble", "shaking", "fade" or ""), `QuakeId`, and afterwards `QuakeFell`.
  - **Physics:** the ground stays still. Every loose piece within 60 of Godling (at most 200) is woken out of its settled state and gets the ground's motion turned round: each tick an impulse of -a(t) × its mass × dt at its centre.
    - The peak is about 0.65 g, in slow swings (0.45, 0.8 and 1.5 Hz).
    - Rapier decides what falls. Settling waits (`Quaking`) until the shaking stops, then squares everything back into cells.
  - **Never moved:** fastened pieces, boulders, terrain, held pieces, and the column of pieces under any body's feet.
  - **Bodies:**
    - `Brace` makes a body crouch and hold on (`Bracing`; the root part is anchored) until 1.5 s after the shaking.
    - At the rumble a body looks around (gesture `LookAround`).
    - A body not braced when the shaking starts is knocked down (`KnockedDown`, gesture `Stagger`) for 2.5 s, then gets up (gesture `GetUp`). It is unhurt.
  - **Gates:**
    - not while one is under way;
    - 10 minutes between disasters in test mode;
    - not while Godling's health is under 30, or within 15 minutes of a faint;
    - at least 15 settled pieces within reach.
  - **The world log** prints `SKY Earthquake #n rumbling / shaking / over: woke W, moved M, fell F`, and `QUAKE knocked … down`.
- **One table for the sky's parts** (`local sky`: `turners`, `power`, `quake`, `pedestal`, `parkGuest`). The main chunk is at Luau's limit of 200 locals; one more stopped the world with "Out of local registers".
- **The guest panel's "Sky" body stays parked.** A character's body does not stay anchored. The parked Sky body fell out of the world and was put back on the spawn spot, on Godling's head (since PR #3; found by the renderer session). It now stands on an invisible `SkyPedestal` at (0, -40, 0) under the island, and is put back there every half second if it drifts.
- **Weather (ADR-010).** Showers every 20–50 minutes, 4–10 long; the Clock carries `Rain` (0–1).
  - Out in the rain, a body walks at 0.65 of its pace and gets soaked (`Wet` 0–100).
  - At night, or soaked in the rain, a body in the open is `Cold`: it loses health and does not heal.
  - `Sheltered` is "roof" when a held piece is overhead (rain kept off). It is "enclosed" when there are also walls on three sides, which with a fire within 7 keeps the cold off.
  - Body attributes: `InRain`, `Wet`, `Cold`, `Sheltered`, and `WalkSpeed` (the pace now).
  - Wet spells of 2–3 in-game days of rain come every 4–7 hours (the first 1.5–2.5 hours in).
  - In heavy rain, lightning strikes a palm near Godling every 3–8 minutes.
  - Fog comes some mornings (the Clock's `Fog`).
  - Rain puts every fire out.
- **Fire by friction (ADR-010).** There is no `Kindle` input.
  - A `Strike` with the Spear on a log rubs it, and rubs in a row heat it (`Heat`).
  - From the 12th rub it may catch, but only with a coconut within 3 as tinder, which burns away.
  - Nothing warms in the rain or for 5 minutes after it.
- **A ship (ADR-010)** sails along the horizon for five minutes every 2.5–5 hours: parts `Ship`, `Ship_Cabin`, `Ship_Mast` and `Ship_Sail`.
- **Driftwood (ADR-010).** Every 3 minutes a `Log_n` marked `Driftwood` washes up on open sand, while fewer than 6 lie on the beach.
- **Renderer (ADR-006).** Godling's own, in `renderer/src/`; it no longer uses the runtime's model helpers.
  - **No CDN.**
    - three.js r187 is bundled in: `npm install`, then `npm run build` (`build.mjs`).
    - The bundle `dist/renderer.bundle.js` is committed. Runs copy the world without `node_modules`, and the build step then keeps the committed bundle.
    - On native Windows `clawblox run` can't find `npm`, and in WSL `npm` may be Windows' own. Either way Clawblox falls back to the committed bundle, so rebuild after changing `src/`.
    - With Clawblox patched (`patches/VIEWER-001`), the page loads nothing from outside the server.
  - **Quality tiers** (`quality.js`): low, medium, high.
    - They set resolution, antialiasing, shadows, the sea's detail and the clouds.
    - The tier is chosen from the device (phone or PC, cores, memory).
    - A software-rendered or automated browser always gets low. That is how Clawblox's `/observe/image` captures Godling's view.
    - The resolution scales down by itself when frames run slow.
  - **Sky and sun** (`sky.js`): three.js's `Sky`.
    - One sun direction drives the light, shadows, fog, clouds and the sea's reflections.
    - By default the sun stands where the light always stood, so the art's checked colours hold.
    - `?time=18` shows that hour; `?dayMinutes=20` runs a day in 20 minutes. A day/night cycle in the world itself is not decided.
  - **Sea** (`sea.js`): it follows the camera to the horizon.
    - The vertex shader makes Gerstner waves.
    - Per pixel: a fresnel blend of water colour and sky, the sun's glint, and foam at the shore.
    - The shore comes from a distance map baked from the Ground and Mesa parts and the terrain.
    - The Ocean part only gives the sea level.
  - **Clouds** (`clouds.js`): Minecraft-style. A flat pixel layer on low, voxel puffs on medium and high.
  - **Drawing parts** (`instances.js`): all parts and static models are instanced, one draw call per kind of mesh.
  - **Sky events:** a `SkyShadow` part is drawn as a dark, see-through disk that casts no shadow.
  - **The castaway island's looks** (set by the world script; read in `index.js`):
    - `Ground` is drawn in the part's colour. With `Surface` "Sand" it is matte sand, where the sea draws its foam.
    - `Mesa` with `Surface` "Rock" is grey volcanic stone in block-high strata, with a moss cap on every step. Other mesas keep the tan stripes.
    - `Wreck` with no model is drawn as a deflated yellow life raft, from its part's size and colour. A `ModelUrl` (`raft.glb`) takes over when set.
  - **Day and night** (ADR-007): the sun follows any entity's `TimeOfDay` attribute (the world's hidden Clock) unless `?time=` or `?dayMinutes=` is set.
    - By night the sky shows a blue glow, pixel stars and a moon opposite the sun.
    - The sun's light becomes cool, dim moonlight. Ambient light turns blue, the sea glints with the moon, and the clouds are moonlit.
  - **Castaway life** (`fire.js`, `fish.js`, `index.js`):
    - **Fire.** `Fire` is pixel flames on three crossed quads and a smoke column about 40 high, bent by a breeze and dark at night. It also has a warm flickering point light. The lights come from a fixed pool (1, 2 or 3 by tier), given to the fires nearest the camera, so lighting a fire never recompiles materials.
    - **Fish.** `Fish` ride the sea's waves (`sea.surfaceAt`, the shader's waves on the CPU) with a dark shape under the surface and a faint wake. Without a model a dorsal fin breaks the surface; with `fish.glb` the model rides half under.
    - **Sand writing.** `SandMark` is drawn at half height with a dark floor and light rims, so a stroke reads as a groove in the sand.
    - **Pieces without models.** `Coconut`, `Volleyball` (with seams), a `Tool` with `ToolKind` "Spear", and `Catch` (browned when `Cooked`) are drawn procedurally until a `ModelUrl` is set.
    - **Ball parts.** Their primitive is `ball` and they draw round.
  - **Godling's animations** (`models.js`, `createCharacterController`):
    - `Gesture` (a clip name) and `Gestures` (a counter) on the player: each new count plays that clip once at its own speed, cutting any one-shot in progress. It is not played on first sight.
    - A model without the clip keeps the older counters: `Reaches` plays PickUp and `Strikes` plays Strike.
    - Jump and Fall loop while the body is jumping or falling, if the model has them. Climb loops while `Climbing` is true.
    - `__godlingRenderer.animations()` lists each character's one-shot and clip weights, for checking a new model.
  - **Models** (`models.js`): each loads once.
    - A prop's "bounds" material (two specks that set its box, from `art/build_props.py`) counts for fitting but is not drawn.
    - Animated ones (Godling) get their own mixer. The clips follow the attributes above, and Walk plays at the body's real speed.
    - In a first-person capture, the body the camera sits in is hidden, so its own head no longer fills Godling's view.
  - **Thought bubble** (`bubble.js`, ADR-009): a Sims cloud over a character whose `Thoughts` counter goes up.
    - It shows a picture of `Thought` (one of the world's 23 keys). The picture is rendered once from the island's own models by a small renderer of its own, or drawn by hand for fire, the HELP sign, a ship, the sea, lightning, a little map (exploring) and the night.
    - Its rim is tinted by `Feeling`: green when glad, red when troubled, grey in between.
    - Each thought pops up, stays 10 s and fades. The bubble is hidden in the body's own first-person view.
  - **Rain** (`rain.js`, ADR-010): falling streaks round the camera while the Clock's `Rain` is up, with dimmer light, greyer fog and heavier clouds. `?rain=1` forces it.
  - **Terrain** (`terrain.js`): Clawblox voxel terrain, drawn as blocks, when the patched server sends it.
  - **Checks.**
    - `?stats=1` shows frame rate, draw calls, triangles and the tier.
    - `?quality=low|medium|high` forces a tier.
    - `globalThis.__godlingRenderer` has the same counts for scripts.

The mind's thinking is not shown in the world. It goes to the run's `mind/thinking.jsonl`, for a separate viewer. Only
the thought bubble's two words (`Thought`, `Feeling`, set with the input `Think`) reach the world.

Everything else (physics, reach, the rock that spawns ImpactBlocks on impact) is upstream's.
