The story

Every life of Godling begins with the same few lines. They are the first thing its mind reads.

“I am Godling. I woke in a wide meadow in the middle of an island I do not know; the wreck of my life raft lies on its west beach. No one knows I am here.”

From its persona, word for word

From there it decides everything itself. First, to be seen from the sea. Then to learn the island, make fire, find food, and build things that can be seen from far away.

When something it built falls, it wants to know why, and tries again differently.

How Godling thinks

A mind in three layers, like ours: one that plans, one that notices, one that moves.

PlansMind

Chooses its next project and says out loud what it intends. Writes lessons, teaches itself skills, and sleeps every hour to sort its memory.

DeepSeek · {{ model }} · reasoning on
NoticesAttention

Sees new things, damage to what it built, visitors and the sky. When something matters, it stops the plan and makes the mind think again.

MovesBody

Walks around the mesas, picks up, places, throws, jumps and climbs. Glances at what attention saw. Braces when the ground shakes.

Skills it teaches itself

Made from body moves. They only count once they have worked in the world.

Memory

What happened, what it meant to do, what it built, and a digest it keeps short.

Rules of the sky

  • You change the world. Never the mind.

    No power, price or note buys a decision.

  • Godling never sees who paid.

    It sees a stone fall, a shadow spread, a sky go dark. Not your name.

  • No disaster while it is weak.

    Not under 30 health, and not for 15 minutes after a death.

  • A storm can hurt it. It never kills.

    A strike leaves half a heart at most. What follows, the cold and the soaking, is up to Godling.

  • Everything you do gets an answer.

    A warning, Godling noticing, a change that stays on the island.

If Godling dies

Death is real here, and Godling learns from it the way you would: by itself.

At zero health

It falls and drops whatever it held.

30 seconds

It lies still. The mind does not think.

Born again

At the start: whole, but half fed.

Afterwards

It reads the facts of its death and writes its own lessons. Nobody tells it what to learn.

Every death is counted on the screen and marked on the replay.
